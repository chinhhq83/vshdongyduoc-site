Editorial Policy

VSH Product Compare publishes consumer-facing product comparisons and scientific evidence summaries to help readers understand product differences, available evidence, and important limitations.

The content is informational and does not replace veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or individualized professional advice.

Source separation

Product and listing information

Product name, brand, listed ingredients, form, product positioning, and Amazon destination are derived from captured Amazon listing data. Missing product facts are not inferred.

Listing statements describe how a product is presented by the seller or manufacturer. They are not treated as independent scientific evidence.

Customer popularity

Customer popularity is a derived relative ranking within the products included in a specific VSH comparison. It may use captured rating, review-volume, and recent-purchase signals internally, but raw values are not published by default.

Customer popularity is not Amazon Best Sellers Rank, a quality score, a scientific evidence score, proof of effectiveness, or a VSH recommendation. If required popularity inputs are incomplete, the public result is shown as unavailable rather than estimated.

Scientific evidence

Scientific evidence is researched independently from Amazon listing statements, customer reviews, and popularity signals. Research is generally performed first at the ingredient, active, or formula-cluster level and is then mapped to products whose listed information supports that mapping.

Evidence concerning an ingredient or related formulation does not prove that the exact commercial product is clinically effective.

Evidence hierarchy

Where relevant evidence is available, VSH gives priority to:

  1. clinical evidence in dogs;
  2. controlled veterinary studies;
  3. systematic reviews and meta-analyses;
  4. other species-relevant clinical evidence.

Human evidence, mechanistic evidence, and laboratory findings may provide context but are not automatically treated as canine clinical outcome evidence. Differences in formulation, dose, duration, population, co-ingredients, and study design are considered when interpreting applicability.

Scientific evidence statuses

VERIFIED_SUPPORTIVE

Relevant evidence provides meaningful support for one or more listed ingredients, actives, or related formulations. This does not establish efficacy of the exact commercial product.

MIXED

Relevant evidence is inconsistent, limited, formulation-dependent, or includes both supportive and non-supportive findings.

INSUFFICIENT

Available evidence is too limited to support a reliable efficacy conclusion. Lack of sufficient evidence should not be interpreted as proof that an ingredient or product is ineffective.

VERIFICATION_REQUIRED

Important information about the ingredient, active, formulation, mapping, or applicability requires additional verification before a stronger scientific conclusion can be assigned.

Product comparison methodology

VSH may compare products using product identity and brand; listed ingredients and form; product focus; campaign-relative price tier; relative customer popularity; scientific evidence status; links to detailed evidence analysis; and Amazon destination.

Price tiers are relative to the captured campaign cohort and should not be interpreted as permanent market price categories.

Reviews and anecdotal experience

Customer reviews, testimonials, and anecdotal reports are not treated as scientific evidence. Review-derived information must not be converted into clinical efficacy claims. Where review data are incomplete, unavailable, or unsuitable for publication, VSH does not invent or reconstruct reviewer conclusions.

Commercial and affiliate relationships

Commercial relationships must not determine which scientific evidence is included, the scientific evidence status, the popularity ranking, or the interpretation of research findings.

Where affiliate links are used, their commercial nature must be disclosed. If VSH is not participating in an affiliate program, links should not be described as affiliate links.

Corrections and updates

Scientific literature, product listings, and market information can change. Readers may report product or listing corrections; scientific or editorial corrections; newly published studies; broken or outdated links; and other material errors through the VSH contact route.

Material corrections should be reviewed and, when appropriate, incorporated into the relevant content with the reviewed or updated date changed accordingly.

Handling uncertainty

VSH does not fill missing information with assumptions. When evidence, identity, or provenance cannot be verified, content should be marked unavailable, marked verification required, withheld from publication, or blocked until verification is complete. The choice depends on whether the unresolved issue affects consumer safety, scientific interpretation, or source integrity.

Authors, reviewers, and publisher

Each indexable evidence or product-analysis page should identify the publisher and, where applicable, the responsible author or reviewer. Reviewer identities, qualifications, and affiliations must be verified before publication. VSH does not fabricate reviewer identities or credentials.

Updates and review dates

Pages should display an appropriate last-reviewed or last-updated date. A page should be reviewed when material product information changes, important new scientific evidence is identified, a correction is accepted, methodology changes, or a material error is discovered.

Structured data and search presentation

VSH uses structured data only where the underlying public information supports the schema properties. VSH does not generate Product, Offer, or AggregateRating schema from incomplete or unlicensed Amazon data.

The goal is to make pages clear, transparent, and citable. VSH does not guarantee that any search engine or AI system will rank, recommend, or cite the content.

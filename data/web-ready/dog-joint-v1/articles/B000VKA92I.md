## Scientific evidence for relevant ingredients/formula
**Evidence status:** MIXED

Canine studies of glucosamine plus chondroitin have produced conflicting results: some earlier trials reported improvement, while later placebo-controlled evidence and a 2022 meta-analysis did not show consistent analgesic benefit. Hyaluronic acid appears in several canine joint-supplement studies, but the evidence reviewed does not establish an independent clinical benefit for oral hyaluronic acid.

### What the evidence does not establish
Formulations, doses and study designs vary, and ingredient-level evidence does not prove that a specific commercial product is effective. Many studies are biomarker-focused or use combination formulas, limiting ingredient-specific conclusions.

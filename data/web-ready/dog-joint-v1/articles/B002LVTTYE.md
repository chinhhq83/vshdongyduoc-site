## Scientific evidence for relevant ingredients/formula
**Evidence status:** MIXED

Several controlled canine studies reported improvement in selected pain, mobility or arthritis measures with green-lipped mussel preparations. MSM is common in canine joint supplements, but the canine-specific standalone clinical evidence identified in this review is insufficient for an independent efficacy conclusion.

### What the evidence does not establish
Extracts, doses and background diets differ across studies, so these findings cannot be assumed to apply to every product containing green-lipped mussel. Human osteoarthritis studies should not be directly extrapolated to dogs, and multi-ingredient products cannot isolate MSM's contribution.

Ingredient- or formula-level evidence does not prove that this exact commercial product is effective.

## Scientific evidence for relevant ingredients/formula
**Evidence status:** MIXED

Randomized canine studies have reported favorable mobility or clinical outcomes with undenatured type II collagen, including UC-II-type preparations. Canine studies of glucosamine plus chondroitin have produced conflicting results: some earlier trials reported improvement, while later placebo-controlled evidence and a 2022 meta-analysis did not show consistent analgesic benefit.

### What the evidence does not establish
The evidence base is still limited, some trials use combination products, and these findings should not be generalized to generic or hydrolyzed collagen. Formulations, doses and study designs vary, and ingredient-level evidence does not prove that a specific commercial product is effective.

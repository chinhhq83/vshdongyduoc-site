# SOURCE AUTHORITY REQUIREMENTS — DOG JOINT V1.3

Goal: make VSH a high-quality, citable source candidate for search/AI systems. No guarantee of ranking, citation or recommendation is made.

Every indexable comparison/evidence/product page should provide, where applicable:
- answer-first summary and one clear intent/entity;
- crawlable semantic text and descriptive headings;
- clear separation of Amazon listing facts, VSH structured analysis and independent scientific evidence;
- visible scientific citations and explicit limitations;
- author/reviewer/publisher attribution;
- last reviewed/updated metadata;
- canonical URL, internal links and sitemap inclusion;
- links to Scientific Methodology, Editorial Policy and correction/contact route;
- supported structured data only, consistent with visible content;
- no fabricated rating/review/Product/Offer schema.

Hard gate: evidence pages cannot be production-ready without verified publisher/reviewer identity, methodology linkage, citations, limitations and a working correction route.

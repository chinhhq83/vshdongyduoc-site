# MSM / oral hyaluronic acid evidence in dogs

## Answer-first summary

**Evidence status: INSUFFICIENT**

These ingredients appear frequently in canine joint formulas, but the available canine clinical evidence located here is mainly from multi-ingredient products rather than trials isolating MSM or oral hyaluronic acid.

## What the evidence does not establish

Combination-product evidence cannot establish the independent effect of MSM or oral hyaluronic acid.

Ingredient/formula evidence does **not** prove that every commercial product containing the ingredient is effective. Dose, formulation, co-ingredients, diagnosis, outcome measure and study design can materially affect applicability.

## Key scientific sources

- Efficacy of a dietary supplement in dogs with osteoarthritis: A randomized placebo-controlled, double-blind clinical trial (2022). PMID 35171954. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0263971. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35171954/

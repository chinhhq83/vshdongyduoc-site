# Avocado/soybean unsaponifiables (ASU) evidence in dogs

## Answer-first summary

**Evidence status: INSUFFICIENT**

Dog research includes experimental structural findings, but species-relevant clinical outcome evidence remains limited for ASU as a stand-alone oral supplement.

## What the evidence does not establish

Experimental induced-osteoarthritis findings are not equivalent to clinical benefit in client-owned dogs.

Ingredient/formula evidence does **not** prove that every commercial product containing the ingredient is effective. Dose, formulation, co-ingredients, diagnosis, outcome measure and study design can materially affect applicability.

## Key scientific sources

- Protective effects of total fraction of avocado/soybean unsaponifiables on the structural changes in experimental dog osteoarthritis (2009). PMID 19291317. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19291317/

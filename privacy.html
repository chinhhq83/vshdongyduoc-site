Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy describes the current data practices of VSH Product Compare, operated by Viện Sinh Hóa Đông Y Dược.

Information you submit

The contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number, and message. We use this information to receive, review, and respond to your inquiry. Please do not submit sensitive medical, veterinary, financial, or other confidential information through the form.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms. When you submit it, the form fields and technical information needed to deliver and protect the submission are sent to that service. Web3Forms acts as a technical service provider for form delivery; its own terms and privacy practices also apply to its processing.

Hosting and audience measurement

The website is hosted using Vercel. Server and network systems may process technical information such as IP address, request time, requested page, browser or device information, and security or diagnostic data to deliver and protect the site. The site also loads Vercel Web Analytics to measure site usage in aggregate, including page views, referral source, device or browser type, and approximate location.

How information is used and shared

We use contact information to respond to inquiries and technical data to operate, secure, diagnose, and understand use of the site. Information may be processed by service providers such as Web3Forms and Vercel for those purposes, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and site integrity. VSH does not sell contact-form information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries, but internet transmission and third-party services cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure or confidential. Contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, maintain related records, resolve issues, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and questions

You may contact VSH to ask a privacy question or request that we review or delete contact information we hold, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. Email vs.hd@vshdongyduoc.org or use the Contact page.

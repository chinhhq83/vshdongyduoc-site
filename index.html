Why VSH is different

We separate product marketing from evidence.

Product listings can tell you what a seller says. They cannot, by themselves, establish that a product works. VSH keeps the evidence layers distinct.

01

Seller information

Captured product identity, formula, format, and other disclosed listing information.

02

VSH analysis

Structured comparison of formula, customer need, price position, and meaningful product differences.

03

Scientific evidence

Independent research is assessed separately and is never inferred from seller claims or popularity.

04

Limitations

Unverified identities, incomplete evidence, and uncertain claims are stated rather than filled in by guesswork.

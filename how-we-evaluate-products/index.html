How We Evaluate Products

VSH separates product information, market signals and scientific evidence so that different kinds of information are not presented as if they mean the same thing.

Product identity and listing information

Product names, brands, ingredients, forms and product focus are based on captured Amazon listing information. Missing information is not inferred. Products with unresolved required publication fields are not published as normal comparison products.

Price tier

Price tiers are defined relative to the captured comparison cohort. They are intended to help shoppers compare market position within the same campaign rather than represent permanent price categories.

Customer popularity

Customer popularity is a derived relative ranking within the comparison. It uses captured market signals but is not Amazon Best Sellers Rank.

Popularity does not mean a product is clinically effective, scientifically stronger, safer or recommended by VSH.

Scientific evidence

Scientific evidence is assessed independently from Amazon listing claims, customer reviews and popularity. Evidence is generally researched by ingredient or formula category and then mapped to relevant products.

Ingredient evidence does not establish that the exact commercial product has been clinically tested.

Affiliate links

Some product links may be affiliate links. Affiliate status does not affect product evaluation and does not determine scientific evidence status or customer popularity ranking.

Decision support

The comparison is designed to help readers narrow choices by considering several independent dimensions: ingredients and form, product focus, price tier, relative customer popularity and scientific evidence.

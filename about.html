About VSH

VSH Product Compare is an educational decision-support project of Viện Sinh Hóa Đông Y Dược (Institute of Biochemistry, Traditional Medicine and Pharmacy).

VSH's broader work focuses on research and the application of scientific methods to traditional medicine and pharmacy. The Dog Joint comparison applies a structured framework to disclosed product information so readers can distinguish listing information, VSH analysis, and independent scientific evidence.

The comparison does not sell veterinary products, provide veterinary diagnosis, or replace individualized advice from a veterinarian. Inclusion in the comparison is not an endorsement.

Transparency

Our Methodology explains how products are grouped, what VSH Assessment means, how relative price tiers work, and the limitations of the dataset. VSH is not currently enrolled in Amazon Associates.

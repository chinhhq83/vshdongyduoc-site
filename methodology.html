Dog Joint Comparison Methodology

This page explains how the VSH Dog Joint comparison is organized, what its assessments mean, and what the current dataset cannot establish.

How products are selected

The current comparison uses a fixed research dataset of dog joint supplements captured from public seller/listing information. Products are included when the dataset contains enough information to place the listing in the comparison framework. Inclusion is not an endorsement, certification, recommendation, or claim that the product is effective. The dataset is not presented as a complete or continuously updated inventory of every product on the market.

What “VSH Assessment” means

A VSH Assessment is a structured decision-support summary derived from the disclosed product information in the captured dataset. It describes the product's stated formula architecture, intended buyer need, comparison question, and relative price tier. It is not a veterinary evaluation, clinical efficacy score, safety determination, product ranking, or guarantee of superiority.

Formula architecture and price tier

Formula architecture groups products by their disclosed ingredient pattern—for example, a conventional glucosamine/chondroitin/MSM structure or a formula built around another disclosed ingredient. These categories help readers compare how listings are positioned; they do not establish ingredient quality, dose adequacy, bioavailability, safety, or clinical benefit.

Price tiers are relative analysis categories assigned within the captured dataset. They are not live Amazon prices, price quotes, or promises that a current seller price falls within a particular range. Prices and availability can change after the dataset is captured.

Three distinct information sources

  • Seller/listing information includes product names, disclosed ingredients, form, claims, and other details captured from a seller or manufacturer listing. VSH does not treat a listing claim as independently proven.
  • VSH analysis is VSH's structured organization and comparison of the captured information. It provides decision framing, not proof of efficacy or superiority.
  • Independent scientific evidence means relevant evidence from sources separate from the seller/listing and assessed on its own merits. Evidence about an ingredient does not automatically prove that a specific finished product is effective.

Amazon customer reviews

Customer-review-derived metrics are not displayed on this site. VSH does not infer product efficacy or superiority from Amazon ratings, review counts, customer reviews, or review quotations. Product assessments are based on the disclosed product information and VSH's structured comparison framework.

Dataset limitations

  • The dataset is a time-bounded capture and may be incomplete, outdated, or affected by changes to a listing.
  • Some product names or listing details may remain unresolved; unresolved information is not guessed.
  • Seller-provided labels and claims may contain omissions or errors and are not independently verified unless explicitly stated.
  • The dataset does not consistently establish current price, availability, manufacturing quality, batch testing, dose suitability, interactions, adverse effects, or outcomes for an individual dog.
  • A formula category cannot substitute for review of the complete current label or consultation with a veterinarian who knows the animal.
Before deciding: Verify current product information with the seller or manufacturer and consult a veterinarian for questions about an individual dog's health, medicines, diet, or suitability for a supplement.

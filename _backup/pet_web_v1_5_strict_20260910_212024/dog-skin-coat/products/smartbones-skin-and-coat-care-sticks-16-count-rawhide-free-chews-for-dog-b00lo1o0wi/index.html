SmartBones Skin And Coat Care Sticks 16 Count, Rawhide-Free Chews For Dogs, With Omega Fatty Acids uses a omega fatty-acid skin & coat chew in soft chew form; the listed 16 count presentation is the concrete listing-level comparison cue. Key check: look for separately disclosed EPA and DHA rather than total omega fats alone; an unspecified omega blend is not evidence-equivalent to a defined marine omega-3 dose. No qualifying visible-review dataset was supplied for this campaign, so VSH does not add a reviewer-experience claim for this ASIN.

Scientific evidence

Evidence status: INSUFFICIENT

Canine studies provide some support for defined EPA/DHA or marine-oil supplementation in skin and coat settings, but an unspecified omega-fatty-acid chew cannot be assumed to deliver an evidence-equivalent omega-3 dose.

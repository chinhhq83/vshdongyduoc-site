Scientific evidence

Evidence status: MIXED

Several canine studies report improvements in atopic or pruritic skin outcomes with specific probiotic or gut-skin interventions, but a recent systematic review and meta-analysis did not find statistically significant pooled effects on standard dermatitis or pruritus scores.

## Scientific evidence for relevant ingredients/formula
**Evidence status:** MIXED

Controlled canine studies and a systematic review support a potential benefit of fish-derived omega-3 fatty acids for osteoarthritis-related pain or mobility, although results are not uniformly positive. A randomized placebo-controlled trial in dogs with naturally occurring osteoarthritis found no improvement in pain, lameness, quality of life or rescue analgesic use with vitamin E.

### What the evidence does not establish
The evidence depends on EPA/DHA dose and formulation; it does not establish that every commercial product containing omega-3 will produce the same result. Earlier experimental-model findings do not establish symptomatic benefit in naturally occurring canine osteoarthritis.

Ingredient- or formula-level evidence does not prove that this exact commercial product is effective.

## Scientific evidence for relevant ingredients/formula
**Evidence status:** MIXED

Canine studies of glucosamine plus chondroitin have produced conflicting results: some earlier trials reported improvement, while later placebo-controlled evidence and a 2022 meta-analysis did not show consistent analgesic benefit. MSM is common in canine joint supplements, but the canine-specific standalone clinical evidence identified in this review is insufficient for an independent efficacy conclusion.

### What the evidence does not establish
Formulations, doses and study designs vary, and ingredient-level evidence does not prove that a specific commercial product is effective. Human osteoarthritis studies should not be directly extrapolated to dogs, and multi-ingredient products cannot isolate MSM's contribution.

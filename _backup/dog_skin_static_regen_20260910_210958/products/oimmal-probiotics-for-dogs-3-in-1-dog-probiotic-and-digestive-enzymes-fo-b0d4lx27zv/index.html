Scientific evidence

Evidence status: MIXED.

Several canine studies report improvements in atopic or pruritic skin outcomes with specific probiotic or gut-skin interventions, but a recent systematic review and meta-analysis did not find statistically significant pooled effects on standard dermatitis or pruritus scores.

What the evidence does not show

Effects are strain-, dose-, duration-, and formulation-specific. Evidence for one probiotic strain or blend cannot be generalized to all commercial probiotic skin products. This evidence does not establish efficacy for the exact product.

The cited literature does not establish that this exact ASIN has been clinically tested, does not verify the seller's marketing wording, and does not predict benefit for an individual dog.

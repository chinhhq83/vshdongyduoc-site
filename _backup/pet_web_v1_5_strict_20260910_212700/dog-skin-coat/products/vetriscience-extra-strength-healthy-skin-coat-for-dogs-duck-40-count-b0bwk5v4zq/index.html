VSH assessment

For buyers prioritizing itch and seasonal-sensitivity support who are comfortable with a treat-style daily routine. VetriScience Extra Strength Healthy Skin & Coat for Dogs, Duck, 40 Count uses a omega + skin nutrient formula in soft chew form; the listed 40 count presentation is the concrete listing-level comparison cue. Key check: look for separately disclosed EPA and DHA rather than total omega fats alone; an unspecified omega blend is not evidence-equivalent to a defined marine omega-3 dose. No qualifying visible-review dataset was supplied for this campaign, so VSH does not add a reviewer-experience claim for this ASIN.

Scientific evidence

Evidence status: INSUFFICIENT

There is canine evidence for selected nutrients and marine omega-3 relevant to skin and coat, but evidence is insufficient to validate this complete multinutrient formula as an exact clinically tested product.

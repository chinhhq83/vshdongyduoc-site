Scientific evidence

Evidence status: MIXED

Canine trials provide some support for omega-3 and polyunsaturated-fatty-acid supplementation in skin and coat contexts, but the evidence does not establish that a specific omega 3-6-9 ratio is superior or consistently effective.

Scientific limitations

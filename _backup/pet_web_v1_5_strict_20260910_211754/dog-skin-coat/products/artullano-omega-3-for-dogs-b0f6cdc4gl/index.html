Scientific evidence

Evidence status: MIXED

Randomized canine studies report improvements in poor coat quality or pruritic/atopic skin outcomes with EPA/DHA or marine-oil supplementation, but veterinary reviews describe the overall evidence as modest and heterogeneous.

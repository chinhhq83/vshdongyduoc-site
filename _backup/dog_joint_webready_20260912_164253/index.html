What this table is for

Fast triage. It is designed to help a buyer identify which products deserve a closer look, rather than pretending one universal ranking fits every dog or buyer.

What “VSH assessment” means

A short explanation of the product's ingredients, form, likely comparison point and relative price tier. It is not a clinical efficacy rating.

Evidence boundary

Seller/listing information, VSH analysis and independent scientific evidence are treated as distinct sources. Customer-review-derived metrics are not displayed, and VSH does not infer efficacy or superiority from Amazon ratings, review counts or customer reviews.

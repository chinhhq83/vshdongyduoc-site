Evidence detail

APPLE_CIDER_VINEGAR: A randomized placebo-controlled canine study found that topical 50/50 vinegar spray did not improve dermatitis, skin pH, or transepidermal water loss. || COLOSTRUM: Insufficient canine clinical evidence was verified to support colostrum for canine skin allergy or pruritus. || TURMERIC_CURCUMIN: Insufficient direct canine clinical evidence was verified for turmeric or curcumin in skin allergy or pruritus.

Scientific limitations

APPLE_CIDER_VINEGAR: The study evaluated topical vinegar and does not establish effects of oral apple-cider-vinegar ingredients. || COLOSTRUM: Mechanistic or immune-support claims should not be treated as clinical efficacy. || TURMERIC_CURCUMIN: General anti-inflammatory evidence does not establish canine dermatologic efficacy.

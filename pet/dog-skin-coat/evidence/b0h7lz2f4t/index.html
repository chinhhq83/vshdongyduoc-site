Evidence detail

BIOTIN: An older canine clinical study reported improvement in skin and coat signs during biotin supplementation. || QUERCETIN: Insufficient canine clinical evidence was verified for quercetin in canine allergic skin disease. || ZINC: A randomized crossover study supports possible adjunctive benefit of zinc methionine in canine atopic dermatitis.

Scientific limitations

BIOTIN: Study lacked a randomized placebo-controlled design, limiting causal conclusions. || QUERCETIN: Mechanistic antihistamine or antioxidant rationale is not equivalent to demonstrated clinical benefit. || ZINC: Intervention also contained essential fatty acids and biotin; results were not uniformly significant and further studies were recommended.

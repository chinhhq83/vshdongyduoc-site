What this status means

Relevant listed ingredients or actives have mixed, limited, or differing levels of scientific support; this does not establish efficacy of the exact product.

Evidence detail

BIOTIN: An older canine clinical study reported improvement in skin and coat signs during biotin supplementation. || VITAMIN_C: No sufficiently strong canine skin-specific clinical evidence was verified for vitamin C in this evidence review. || VITAMIN_E: A placebo-controlled canine study reported significantly lower CADESI scores with adjunctive vitamin E supplementation. || ZINC: A randomized crossover study supports possible adjunctive benefit of zinc methionine in canine atopic dermatitis.

Scientific limitations

BIOTIN: Study lacked a randomized placebo-controlled design, limiting causal conclusions. || VITAMIN_C: Presence in a formula should not be interpreted as proven skin or anti-allergy efficacy. || VITAMIN_E: Evidence base is small and does not prove efficacy of every vitamin-E-containing formula. || ZINC: Intervention also contained essential fatty acids and biotin; results were not uniformly significant and further studies were recommended.

Zesty Paws Dog Vitamins - Dog Multivitamins, Mini, Chicken, 90 Ct: scientific evidence

MIXED — Relevant listed ingredients or actives have mixed, limited, or differing levels of scientific support; this does not establish efficacy of the exact product.

What this status means

Relevant listed ingredients or actives have mixed, limited, or differing levels of scientific support; this does not establish efficacy of the exact product.

Evidence detail

BIOTIN: An older canine clinical study reported improvement in skin and coat signs during biotin supplementation. || PROBIOTIC: A 2025 meta-analysis found no significant pooled improvement in canine atopic dermatitis, while a later randomized LGG study reported improved clinical outcomes. || VITAMIN_C: No sufficiently strong canine skin-specific clinical evidence was verified for vitamin C in this evidence review. || VITAMIN_E: A placebo-controlled canine study reported significantly lower CADESI scores with adjunctive vitamin E supplementation.

Scientific limitations

BIOTIN: Study lacked a randomized placebo-controlled design, limiting causal conclusions. || PROBIOTIC: Effects may be strain-specific; evidence for one probiotic strain cannot be transferred to generic probiotic blends. || VITAMIN_C: Presence in a formula should not be interpreted as proven skin or anti-allergy efficacy. || VITAMIN_E: Evidence base is small and does not prove efficacy of every vitamin-E-containing formula.

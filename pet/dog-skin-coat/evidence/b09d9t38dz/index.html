What this status means

Supportive scientific evidence exists for one or more listed ingredients or actives relevant to this product's skin/coat focus; this does not prove efficacy of the exact product.

Evidence detail

OMEGA3_EPA_DHA: Randomized canine studies support potential benefit of marine omega-3/EPA-DHA or PUFA supplementation for atopic dermatitis and pruritus, although response magnitude and formulations vary.

Scientific limitations

OMEGA3_EPA_DHA: Ingredient-level evidence only; does not prove every fish-oil product works or establish equivalence between doses/formulations.

Evidence detail

PREBIOTIC: Insufficient canine skin-specific clinical evidence was verified for prebiotics as an independent intervention. || PROBIOTIC: A 2025 meta-analysis found no significant pooled improvement in canine atopic dermatitis, while a later randomized LGG study reported improved clinical outcomes. || VITAMIN_E: A placebo-controlled canine study reported significantly lower CADESI scores with adjunctive vitamin E supplementation. || ZINC: A randomized crossover study supports possible adjunctive benefit of zinc methionine in canine atopic dermatitis.

Scientific limitations

PREBIOTIC: Gut-health rationale or inclusion with probiotics is not proof of skin efficacy. || PROBIOTIC: Effects may be strain-specific; evidence for one probiotic strain cannot be transferred to generic probiotic blends. || VITAMIN_E: Evidence base is small and does not prove efficacy of every vitamin-E-containing formula. || ZINC: Intervention also contained essential fatty acids and biotin; results were not uniformly significant and further studies were recommended.

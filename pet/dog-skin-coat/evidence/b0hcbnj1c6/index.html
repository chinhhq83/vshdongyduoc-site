Evidence detail

COLOSTRUM: Insufficient canine clinical evidence was verified to support colostrum for canine skin allergy or pruritus. || PROBIOTIC: A 2025 meta-analysis found no significant pooled improvement in canine atopic dermatitis, while a later randomized LGG study reported improved clinical outcomes. || TURMERIC_CURCUMIN: Insufficient direct canine clinical evidence was verified for turmeric or curcumin in skin allergy or pruritus. || VITAMIN_C: No sufficiently strong canine skin-specific clinical evidence was verified for vitamin C in this evidence review.

Scientific limitations

COLOSTRUM: Mechanistic or immune-support claims should not be treated as clinical efficacy. || PROBIOTIC: Effects may be strain-specific; evidence for one probiotic strain cannot be transferred to generic probiotic blends. || TURMERIC_CURCUMIN: General anti-inflammatory evidence does not establish canine dermatologic efficacy. || VITAMIN_C: Presence in a formula should not be interpreted as proven skin or anti-allergy efficacy.

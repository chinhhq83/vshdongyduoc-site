Native Pet Skin & Coat Chews - Omega 3 Fish Oil for Dogs Skin & Coat Supplement - Itch Relief, Shiny Fur & Shedding Support - Collagen, Hyaluronic Acid, Biotin, Probiotics & Salmon Oil - 120 Chews: scientific evidence

What this status means

Relevant listed ingredients or actives have mixed, limited, or differing levels of scientific support; this does not establish efficacy of the exact product.

Evidence detail

BIOTIN: An older canine clinical study reported improvement in skin and coat signs during biotin supplementation. || COLLAGEN: Canine collagen clinical evidence identified in this review concerns joint outcomes rather than skin or coat efficacy. || HYALURONIC_ACID: Insufficient canine skin-specific clinical evidence was verified for hyaluronic acid. || OMEGA3_EPA_DHA: Randomized canine studies support potential benefit of marine omega-3/EPA-DHA or PUFA supplementation for atopic dermatitis and pruritus, although response magnitude and formulations vary. || PROBIOTIC: A 2025 meta-analysis found no significant pooled improvement in canine atopic dermatitis, while a later randomized LGG study reported improved clinical outcomes. || VITAMIN_E: A placebo-controlled canine study reported significantly lower CADESI scores with adjunctive vitamin E supplementation. || ZINC: A randomized crossover study supports possible adjunctive benefit of zinc methionine in canine atopic dermatitis.

Scientific limitations

BIOTIN: Study lacked a randomized placebo-controlled design, limiting causal conclusions. || COLLAGEN: Human skin collagen trials cannot be directly extrapolated to dogs. || HYALURONIC_ACID: Joint evidence or human dermatology evidence does not establish canine skin efficacy. || OMEGA3_EPA_DHA: Ingredient-level evidence only; does not prove every fish-oil product works or establish equivalence between doses/formulations. || PROBIOTIC: Effects may be strain-specific; evidence for one probiotic strain cannot be transferred to generic probiotic blends. || VITAMIN_E: Evidence base is small and does not prove efficacy of every vitamin-E-containing formula. || ZINC: Intervention also contained essential fatty acids and biotin; results were not uniformly significant and further studies were recommended.

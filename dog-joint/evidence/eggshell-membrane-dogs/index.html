Eggshell membrane evidence in dogs

Answer-first summary

Evidence status: INSUFFICIENT

Canine studies provide promising but still limited evidence; an earlier pilot was not definitive, and a newer positive trial tested a multi-ingredient formula rather than eggshell membrane alone.

What the evidence does not establish

Small studies and combination products make it difficult to isolate the contribution of eggshell membrane.

Ingredient/formula evidence does not prove that every commercial product containing the ingredient is effective. Dose, formulation, co-ingredients, diagnosis, outcome measure and study design can materially affect applicability.

Key scientific sources

  • Placebo-controlled pilot study of the effects of an eggshell membrane-based supplement on mobility and serum biomarkers in dogs with osteoarthritis (2019). PMID 31685140. DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2019.105379. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31685140/
  • Efficacy of a joint supplement containing eggshell membrane among other ingredients to improve the mobility of dogs with osteoarthritis (2025). PMID 40530040. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40530040/

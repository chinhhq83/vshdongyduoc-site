Undenatured type II collagen (UC-II) evidence in dogs

Answer-first summary

Evidence status: VERIFIED_SUPPORTIVE

Several canine controlled studies report mobility or pain-related improvements with undenatured type II collagen, including trials of UC-II alone or in combination.

What the evidence does not establish

The evidence base is smaller than for established veterinary therapies; some trials use active comparators or combination formulas, limiting product-level generalization.

Ingredient/formula evidence does not prove that every commercial product containing the ingredient is effective. Dose, formulation, co-ingredients, diagnosis, outcome measure and study design can materially affect applicability.

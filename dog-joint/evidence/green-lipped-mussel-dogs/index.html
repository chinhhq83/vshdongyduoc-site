Green-lipped mussel evidence in dogs

Answer-first summary

Evidence status: VERIFIED_SUPPORTIVE

Several controlled canine studies have reported improvement in selected arthritis or mobility measures with green-lipped mussel preparations.

What the evidence does not establish

Preparations, doses and outcome measures differ across studies, and results from a studied extract or diet should not be assumed for every commercial product.

Ingredient/formula evidence does not prove that every commercial product containing the ingredient is effective. Dose, formulation, co-ingredients, diagnosis, outcome measure and study design can materially affect applicability.

Key scientific sources

  • Influence of green lipped mussels (Perna canaliculus) in alleviating signs of arthritis in dogs (2003). PMID 15136981. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15136981/
  • Evaluating Complementary Therapies for Canine Osteoarthritis Part I: Green-lipped Mussel (Perna canaliculus) (2009). PMID 18955269. DOI: 10.1093/ecam/nem136. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18955269/

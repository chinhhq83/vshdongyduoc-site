Scientific Methodology

VSH evaluates scientific evidence separately from Amazon listing information, customer popularity and affiliate considerations.

Evidence scope

Research is assessed primarily at the ingredient or formula-cluster level. Evidence for an ingredient does not prove that every commercial product containing that ingredient has been clinically tested or will produce the same result.

Evidence preference

Priority is given to species-relevant clinical evidence in dogs where available. Human studies, mechanistic evidence and experimental studies may provide context but are not treated as equivalent to canine clinical outcome evidence.

Evidence interpretation

VSH distinguishes the certainty of the evidence from the direction or strength of support. A formula category may have substantial research but still show mixed, inconsistent or limited clinical benefit.

Scientific evidence statuses

  • VERIFIED_SUPPORTIVE — relevant evidence provides meaningful support.
  • MIXED — findings are inconsistent or conflicting.
  • INSUFFICIENT — evidence is too limited for a confident conclusion.
  • VERIFICATION_REQUIRED — evidence or product mapping requires further verification.
  • NOT_ASSESSED — no scientific assessment has yet been completed.

Important limitations

Study formulation, dose, duration, ingredient quality, co-ingredients, dog population and outcome measures may differ from commercially available products.

Scientific evidence is therefore presented as decision support rather than as proof that a specific ASIN is effective.
